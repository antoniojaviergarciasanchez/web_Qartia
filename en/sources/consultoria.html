Consulting and turnkey project

From need to deployment

We translate a technical challenge into a clear, executable solution prepared for growth.

Qartia accompanies companies and institutions from the technical definition of the project to its actual deployment, combining sensors, communications, electronics, artificial intelligence and systems integration.

This line is aimed at projects where you have to choose technology, design architecture, resolve coverage, select devices and land an executable roadmap. The objective is to convert a technical need into a deployable, scalable solution aligned with the client's operations.

We can enter into early diagnostic phases or take on the complete journey through installation, commissioning and subsequent evolution.

Qartia consulting connects coverage, sensors, visualization and operational exploitation of the data.

Technological selection

Technical definition

Architecture, coverage and scope before purchasing or installing.

Qartia helps make decisions about what to measure, how to connect it, what topology is best, and how to integrate the solution with the client's operation.

Turnkey project

From architecture to functioning system.

When the project requires it, Qartia goes from definition to execution: electronics, integration, private network, installation, dashboards and validation of the solution in real conditions.

  • Integration of devices, panels, energy and communications.
  • Visualization, alerts, AI and data exploitation.
  • Phased scaling without redoing the project foundation.
