Industrial, environmental and agricultural sensorization

Data capture

We design measurement systems that allow data to be captured, transmitted and arrive ready to operate

Qartia designs and deploys sensorization solutions for industrial, environmental, agricultural and civil environments, adapting the measurement system to the operational reality of each project.

This line integrates selection of sensors, electronics, power, connectivity, mechanical protection and data visualization. The objective is to capture reliable information in the field and turn it into a solid basis for monitoring, alerts, predictive maintenance or decision-making support.

We do not work only with the sensor. We design the complete set: support, case, power, communications, capture logic and platform so that the system can be maintained, scaled and coexist with the client's real operation.

Robust sensorization for continuous operation in agricultural, environmental and industrial environments.

What we solve

Useful sensorization is not a list of variables: it is a complete measurement architecture.

Industry

Monitoring of machines, processes, utilities and assets where the data must coexist with the plant operation.

Environment

Gas, particle, noise and meteorological variable networks for continuous monitoring and public or operational visualization.

Agriculture and agriculture

Crop sensors, microclimate, irrigation, radiation and agricultural exploitation for productive control and resource efficiency.

Demanding environments

Design of boxes, power, supports and communications for outdoors, electrical noise, anti-vandalism or complex access.

From the sensor to the dashboard

Each deployment combines measurement, protection, connectivity and data exploitation.

Selection and integration

Sensors, electronics and acquisition adapted to each variable.

Qartia works with industrial, environmental and agricultural sensors, integrating the variable, range, sampling frequency and operating logic in electronics and firmware consistent with the end use.

  • Analog and digital capture, specific sensors and measurement electronics.
  • Sampling frequency and data quality defined according to use case.
  • Preparation for alerts, AI, predictive maintenance or reporting.
Field deployment

Mechanical protection, autonomy and communications in the same technical base.

The sensorization of Qartia does not remain in the laboratory. It is deployed in a plant, city or territory with energy, support, cash, private network and backhaul ready for a stable service over time.

  • Field boxes, cabinets, power and solarization when necessary.
  • LoRa, DECT NR+, 5G, Ethernet or hybrid architectures depending on the scenario.
  • Integration with dashboards, alarms, databases and existing systems.
Key capabilities

A sensorization base designed to operate, integrate and grow.

Sensor selection

Variables, range, precision, materiality and conditions of use aligned with the real case.

Acquisition electronics

Hardware design and integration to capture useful signal and maintain stability over time.

Firmware and local logic

Configuration of sampling cycles, alarms, data packaging and energy management.

Protection and mechanics

Boxes, supports, anti-vandalism, panel integration and displays prepared for outdoors or plant.

Private network and backhaul

LoRa, DECT NR+, 5G, Ethernet and Internet output according to coverage, latency and operation.

Platform and visualization

Dashboards, maps, indicators and data exploitation for operation, monitoring and decision.

Alerts and automation

Rules, thresholds and event logic to transform the measurement into operational action.

Phased scaling

Architecture ready to start with a pilot and grow without redoing the entire system.
