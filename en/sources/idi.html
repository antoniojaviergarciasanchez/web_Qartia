R&D&i

Applied research

We develop customized solutions and convert knowledge into a product, pilot and transfer.

At Qartia we develop ad hoc engineering solutions in environmental monitoring, sensors, communications and artificial intelligence based on the specifications of the industrial, agricultural, civil or public administration client.

We have more than 20 years of experience developing private R&D projects and with administrations, as coordinators or partners in regional, national and European projects.

The objective of this line is to take a technical idea from applied research and prototype to a validated pilot or a solution transferable to a real environment.

Qartia takes R&D&i from prototype to validation in the plant and transfer to product.
