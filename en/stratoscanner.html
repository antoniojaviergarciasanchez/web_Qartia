StratoScanner

Portable DECT NR+ spectrum analyzer for identifying occupied channels between 1880 and 1900 MHz and displaying received RSSI power in the field.

New product

Portable DECT NR+ spectrum measurement with immediate reading.

StratoScanner is designed to measure the full bandwidth allowed by the European ETSI TS 103 636 standard, from 1880 to 1900 MHz, and show which channels are occupied and the power at which packets are received.

Once initialized, the device performs a continuous 50 ms scan per channel. Packets are received through an SMA antenna, processed by the nRF9161 microchip and shown on the integrated OLED display for field reading.
