Civil Infrastructures

Connected infrastructure

Intelligent monitoring for tunnels, bridges, viaducts, dams and critical infrastructures.

Qartia designs, develops and integrates IoT systems, wireless communications, advanced analytics and Artificial Intelligence models to know the status of critical civil infrastructures in real time.

The solution allows you to capture structural, environmental and operational variables, send them safely to the platform and convert them into dashboards, risk thresholds and actionable alerts for operators and technical managers.

This line is especially relevant for tunnels, bridges, viaducts, roads, dams, hospitals, unique buildings and hydraulic infrastructures where service continuity and safety depend on continuous supervision.

With Qartia, the infrastructure stops being managed only by specific inspection and begins to function as a monitored, traceable asset prepared for predictive maintenance.

What Qartia does in this type of deployments

Qartia integrates sensors, data acquisition, communications, power, visualization and intelligence to turn the physical asset into a monitored operating system.

  • Tailored technical design according to the asset, criticality and needs of the operator.
  • Digitization of physical and environmental signals with its own acquisition and transmission electronics.
  • Wireless network integration, backhaul, Cloud server Qartia and centralized web visualization.
  • Scalable architecture for tunnels, bridges, viaducts, dams and hydraulic infrastructures.

Operating result

The client obtains a continuously monitored infrastructure, with the ability to contextualize events and scale the deployment without redoing the architecture.

  • Visibility of the asset status in real time.
  • Traceability of incidents and field events.
  • Technical basis for predictive maintenance and future expansion.

Connected data from field to operation.

The platform is supported by field acquisition, IoT network, secure backhaul, Qartia cloud and intelligent models to transform technical measurement into preventive operation.

  • Conversion of analog and digital signals from structural and environmental sensors.
  • LoRa, 5G, MQTT and secure VPN according to coverage and criticality scenario.
  • Web visualization, digital twin, risk thresholds and business rules.
  • Alerts and notifications for operation, maintenance and emergency services.

Operational layers that integrate
