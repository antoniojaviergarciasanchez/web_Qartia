Critical Asset Monitoring

Smart industry

Continuous monitoring of critical industrial assets.

Qartia Smart Technologies SL drives the digital transformation of the industry through advanced intelligent plant monitoring solutions, designed to maximize the availability and performance of critical assets.

Our technology allows continuous and real-time monitoring directly in industry, integrating high-precision sensors in machinery, auxiliary services and productive infrastructures to obtain real operational data in real time.

This approach improves visibility, control and traceability over equipment and processes, facilitating early warnings, dashboards and faster decisions by operations and maintenance.

As a differential value, Qartia combines plant monitoring with its own IoT infrastructure and decentralized and non-cellular 5G networks, allowing it to operate even in environments without infrastructure and guaranteeing reliable connectivity for critical applications.

With Qartia, companies move towards more autonomous and resilient management of their critical assets, with better operational continuity, greater security and more capacity for anticipation.

Real case

Design and development of a remote monitoring system for critical assets at ILBOC.

Qartia developed for ILBOC (Iberian Lube Base Oils Company S.A.) a remote monitoring system for critical assets aimed at improving the operation, maintenance and safety of strategic plant equipment.

The case focused on the remote supervision of a fire engine located in the Escombreras Valley, Cartagena, integrating data collection, communications and visualization in the same solution.

  • Connection to the communication buses of the pump sensors and to electrical signals related to the status of the system and its service conditions.
  • Robust platform for data capture and communications between the asset and the central offices, separated by several kilometers.
  • Alert and notification service for operation and maintenance.
  • Intuitive graphical interface to visualize the status of the asset and facilitate decision making.
