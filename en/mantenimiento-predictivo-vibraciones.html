Predictive Maintenance. Vibrations

Industrial vibrations

Continuous diagnosis for predictive maintenance.

Qartia Smart Technologies SL develops predictive maintenance solutions, in real time, based on advanced vibration analysis to anticipate failures in rotating machinery and industrial assets.

Our technology captures high-resolution signal directly in the plant, continuously analyzes the condition and transforms vibration data into actionable alerts for operations, maintenance and engineering.

The system early detects misalignments, imbalances, bearing wear, lack of lubrication and other degradation patterns before they cause unplanned shutdowns.

Qartia integrates sensors, proprietary electronics, private networks DECT NR+ and Artificial Intelligence models to deploy an end-to-end solution, even in complex industrial environments or without prior communications infrastructure.

The result is a more autonomous maintenance model, with lower operational risk, better OEE and greater traceability of the status of each asset.

Technical architecture

Edge + private network + analytics.

The technical architecture of Qartia is supported by proprietary hardware, DECT NR+ communications and an expert diagnostic engine with flexible deployment.
