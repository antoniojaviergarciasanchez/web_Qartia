Environment

Connected environment

Continuous environmental monitoring for air and noise.

Qartia helps turn environmental monitoring into a daily work tool. We do it by combining IoT sensor technology, connectivity, dashboards and analytics so that the data arrives well and serves to decide.

We monitor, in real time, WHO criterion gases, particles, noise, temperature, humidity and other variables continuously, relating all this information to detect changes, issue warnings and better understand what is happening in each environment.

Projects such as Cabezo Beaza (Cartagena), Atlantic Copper (Huelva) and the Cartagena City Council show this approach: deploy in the field, collect the data with stability and bring it to a clear visualization for operation and monitoring.

In the field, communications are supported by a private network (LoRa/DECT NR+) and access to the Internet can be done through 5G, WiFi or Ethernet networks. This way the solution adapts better to each location and can grow in phases.

Finally, we have developed AI tools to predict, based on data from our sensors together with other already installed ones, for example local, regional or state measuring stations, future values ​​of contaminants and, if appropriate, launch alerts.
