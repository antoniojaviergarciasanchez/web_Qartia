Biosentia

Intelligent electrochemical sensing platform for health, sports, nutrition and portable diagnosis.

Product Qartia

Intelligent sensing to measure concentrations of different biochemical substances in real time.

Biosentia is an advanced electronic and communication platform aimed at the development of electrochemical analysis solutions applied to health, sports, the food industry, biotechnology and portable molecular diagnosis.

The product integrates hardware design, firmware implementation, the acquisition of very low intensity electrochemical signals (up to nanoamperes), data processing and wireless connectivity into a single ecosystem, allowing complex chemical and biochemical assays to be transformed into compact, reliable systems adapted to different use environments.

  • Modular and scalable architecture for desktop, laptop and wearable.
  • Compatible with screen-printed electrodes, disposable sensors and epidermal or intradermal needles.
  • Electrochemical measurement electronics, firmware, BLE connectivity and integration IoT.
  • Prepared for prototypes, pilots and application-specific pre-industrial developments.
  • Measurements in saliva, serum, urine or subcutaneously, among others, where the concentrations of the analyte under study may be negligible.

Biosentia converts electrochemical and molecular measurement platforms into compact, connected and ready devices for clinical, sports, food or field scenarios.

Application lines

One platform, four lines of development.

Biosentia Health Solutions aimed at the detection of biomarkers of clinical and physiological interest, such as cholesterol, ketone bodies and hormones related to fertility (Estradiol, Progesterone, Luteinizing and Prolactin) through saliva samples. In the latter case, with this device, the doctor, through this sample of the patient's saliva, and without having to travel to the infertility clinic, knows the best time for the embryo transfer. This range of portable devices allows reliable analysis in clinical, healthcare, home or field environments.
Biosentia Food Quality control systems for the food industry aimed at measuring ascorbic acid and other relevant compounds in foods and beverages. Transfer electrochemical analysis to process control with compact and fast solutions.
Biosentia Sport & Wear Real-time physiological monitoring using portable devices and miniaturized wearables. It allows measuring variables associated with performance, fatigue, metabolism, recovery and the user's physiological state with remote visualization and analysis.
Biosentia Gene Portable platform for molecular diagnosis based on LAMP techniques, aimed at the rapid detection of genetic material in biological samples. It offers a precision comparable to a PCR, with a more compact, connected and lower cost solution, prepared for point of care, screening or mobile laboratory.
Technological features

Electrochemistry, firmware and connectivity in a single technological base.

  • Custom electrochemical electronics.
  • Design of potentiostats, potentiometers and signal acquisition systems.
  • Support for cyclic voltammetry, chronoamperometry and potentiometry.
  • Embedded firmware configurable according to application.
  • Bluetooth Low Energy Communication — BLE.
  • Integration with IoT platforms for remote monitoring.
  • Data processing, visualization and storage.
Differential value

A flexible platform for different analytes, matrices and scenarios.

Biosentia's differential value lies in its ability to unite electronics, electrochemistry, biotechnology and connectivity in a single technological platform. Instead of being limited to a closed trial, Biosentia allows the development of specific devices for different analytes and contexts of use.

This makes it a useful base for research, technological validation, industrial transfer and development of new products, evolving from portable point analysis systems to continuous monitoring wearables or molecular diagnosis solutions with remote connectivity.
